① Match

Agree where every old field goes and what it becomes (data mapping). Start from your supplier's mapping template, or rebuild the old system's field list from screenshots if access has ended. AI suggests; people decide.

② Test

Track every field through the test load and the live load. Your team checks each one on screen and records pass or fail, by name. Failures stay in a problems log until they pass. Have the files? Compare record counts and totals too.

③ Sign off

Each area's manager signs off, with a warning if anything is still open. A one-page executive overview for the board, and an evidence pack for audit.

Common questions

Do you move the data?

No. We help you and your supplier agree which fields match. Your supplier writes the queries that move the data.

Do you need access to our personal data?

Not to match fields: that uses field names and types only. For the optional file comparison, MapBridge reads extract files that contain real records, but only in your own browser. The rows are never saved or sent to us or any server, and fields marked as personal are masked in reports.

Where is the data stored?

In your web browser on your computer. Not on a server. When you close the browser, the data stays on your computer unless you clear your browser data.

What systems do you work with?

We work with finance, HR, housing, CRM, care and schools systems. The read-only connector supports SQL Server and Oracle. For other systems, we upload field lists as CSV or JSON.

How long does a system change take?

It depends on the size of your system and how much time you have before go-live. A typical project is 6 to 12 months. Start before your contract ends if you can.
