Five stages, one team

We run your system change from the day you know the contract is ending to signed-off proof after go-live. You work with us directly. MapBridge keeps every field matched, tested and signed off.

Book a 30-minute call See the stages ↓

Most system changes fail between suppliers. Old supplier, new supplier, and your team in the middle. We own the whole journey so that does not happen.

1

Contract ending

MapBridge

What happens

We help you decide whether to extend, re-procure or move. We map what you have today, what you need, and how long you really have.

What you get

A clear options paper and a rough plan with dates.

Who does what

We lead. Your service areas and IT feed in. Your current supplier answers factual questions only.

Typical timing

2 to 6 weeks, depending on how soon the contract ends.

MapBridge used here

To list every field in the current system so the options paper is based on real data, not guesses.

2

Requirements

What happens

Every service area signs up to what the new system must do. We turn that into a requirements pack a bidder can price.

What you get

A signed requirements pack and a field list ready for procurement.

Who does what

Service leads own their requirements. We facilitate and write them up. Procurement owns the formal process.

Typical timing

4 to 8 weeks.

3

Procurement

What happens

We help you test bidders on your real fields, not a demo script. Evaluation criteria include data migration approach and proof on your data.

What you get

Evaluation notes and a recommendation you can take to members or the board.

Who does what

Procurement runs the process. We support technical and data evaluation. You decide.

Typical timing

Set by your standing orders, often 8 to 16 weeks.

4

Implementation

MapBridge

What happens

Match every old field to the new system. Translate codes. Test and retest. Sign off each area before go-live.

What you get

A live mapping workbook, starter SQL for your supplier, and a sign-off record.

Who does what

We run the mapping and assurance. Your supplier moves the data. Your service leads sign off.

Typical timing

3 to 9 months, sized to the system.

MapBridge used here

Core tool for matching, translations, sign-off history and starter SQL.

5

Go-live and proof

MapBridge

What happens

Cutover plan, parallel checks where needed, and a signed-off record for auditors and members. Then we hand over.

What you get

Go-live proof pack and a clear handover.

Who does what

We coordinate assurance. Supplier and your IT run cutover. You sign the final record.

Typical timing

2 to 6 weeks around go-live.

MapBridge used here

Holds the final signed-off mapping as the audit trail.
