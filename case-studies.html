Case Study · UK Council

Replacing a schools system against the clock

4

months from procurement to go-live

2

people, 12-month project

8

months running test and live side by side

The situation

Procurement finished late. The team had about 4 months before go-live on a project that needed closer to 12. Two people ran the migration. Test and live had to run side by side after the forced go-live. The team was handling lots of live support tickets while still running the programme.

What we learned

Start procurement early enough that implementation is not crushed into the last quarter. When the clock wins, you pay in parallel running, rework and risk.

What we did

Owned the field mapping and assurance between council and supplier. Matched, tested and retested fields. Left a record of who checked what.

Takeaway for a Transformation Director

If your contract ends in the next 18 months, start the decision now. The cost of a late start shows up as parallel running, not as a neat line in the business case.

Caerphilly Council · Occupational Health

Migrating employee health system to the cloud

The problem

The supplier stopped supporting their on-premise system. They built a new cloud platform and were moving all customers over to it. The council needed to migrate without impacting employee occupational health clinics.

What we did

  • Contract negotiation: Helped the council agree favourable terms for a cloud migration in four months. Something that had not been achieved in four years.
  • Testing and configuration: Worked with the client and end users to make sure data migration was accurate and configuration reflected their needs.
  • Risk management: Addressed risks as they arose and found acceptable solutions to keep the project on track.
  • One team approach: Made sure insight and knowledge of each team member was shared and listened to. This helped build a high trust environment.

The result

Successfully migrated from on-premise to Cority Cloud Platform. Employee occupational health clinics were not impacted by the migration.

Caerphilly Council · Human Resources

Moving HR platform to the cloud

The problem

Human resources is the backbone of every business. The existing on-premise system was slow and did not include the latest suite of modules that helps support all HR functions.

What we did

  • Holding supplier to account: Ensured delivery was in line with agreed contract terms. Project tasks and milestones were met. Used escalation routes when needed.
  • Budget management: Took over day-to-day budget for the project to avoid supplier consultancy days exceeding contract terms.
  • Managing up: Created a custom template that clearly communicated whether we were on track and progress against plan.

The result

Successfully migrated iTrent Human Resources platform from on-premise to cloud.

Caerphilly Council · Schools

Migrating 85 schools to the cloud

The problem

ESS SIMS decided to move from an on-premise solution to the cloud. All schools were being encouraged to transition to their interim solution in preparation for SIMS Next Gen. The council needed to migrate 85 schools.

What we did

  • Sustainable pace: Migrated schools in batches of 5-6 with a repeatable and stable process.
  • Learn fast and refine: Schools lacked migration experience. The process emerged by working with early adopters.
  • Frequent, transparent communication: Everyone felt included, heard and listened to. People were more willing to be flexible and help each other.
  • Trust: Demonstrating openness in the project encouraged people to be themselves. Meaningful connection became possible and delivery more achievable.

The result

Successfully migrated 85 schools from on-premise to the cloud.
