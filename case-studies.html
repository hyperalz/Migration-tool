Case Study · UK Council

Replacing a schools system against the clock

4

months from procurement to go-live

2

people, 12-month project

8

months running test and live side by side

The situation

Procurement finished late. The team had about 4 months before go-live on a project that needed closer to 12. Two people ran the migration. Test and live had to run side by side after the forced go-live. The team was handling lots of live support tickets while still running the programme.

What we learned

Start procurement early enough that implementation is not crushed into the last quarter. When the clock wins, you pay in parallel running, rework and risk.

What we did

Owned the field mapping and assurance between council and supplier. Matched, tested and retested fields. Left a record of who checked what.

Takeaway for a Transformation Director

If your contract ends in the next 18 months, start the decision now. The cost of a late start shows up as parallel running, not as a neat line in the business case.
